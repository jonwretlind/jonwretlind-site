        <h2>Truth and Beauty In an Artificial Age</h2>
        <h3>An Exploration of the Philosohpical Concepts of Truth and Beauty In a World Dominated by Artificial Intelligence</h3>
        <p>We live in an age where almost everything seems to be artificial, and our lives are engineered for an artificial reality — one filled with counterfeits and deepfakes in order to pursue a consumer-driven agenda . It is hard to know what is authentic, what is true, and ultimately what is beautiful in the world since we cannot trust our senses to tell us any longer. This thesis explores these concepts as it relates to art and imagery generated by AI, and it seeks to provide some ethical direction for artists working with AI tools today.</p>
