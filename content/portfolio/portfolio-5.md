        <h2>Sentinels</h2>
        <h3>Title Spread</h3>
        <p>"Sentinels" is a photo/poetry project I put together during the COVID lockdowns of 2020. I documented the clock towers and church spires in my neighborhood with pinhole, vintage and homemade cameras on paper and film. These images are representative of the quiet "watchers" over the city when people were forced into their homes and the streets were quiet. The original poetry is rendered with experimental typography to offset each set of images.</p>
      </div>
      <div id="description2">
        <h2>Sentinels 1</h2>
        <p>Downtown clocks tick out the minutes,<br/>
        their towers impatiently marking the hours,</br>
        the tentative moments of life on pause..</p>
      </div>
      <div id="description3">
        <h2>Sentinels 2</h2>
        <p>Church towers silently stand waiting,</br>
        as if reciting liturgies of seasons blurred</br>
        by tears of separation and griefs unresolved.</p>
      </div>
      <div id="description4">
        <h2>Sentinels 3</h2>
        <p>These towering sentinels keep their silent watch,</br>
        in hope to chime a brighter day with bombastic bells</br>
        but cannot, for no one hears them ring.</p>
      </div>
      <div id="description5">
        <h2>Sentinels 4</h2>
        <p>Time, measured in minutes, hours and seasons</br>
        and in the death of all we trusted to be eternal,</br>
        we mark time acting as if nothing truly changes.</p>
      </div>
      <div id="description6">
        <h2>Sentinels 5</h2>
        <p>Until the clocks tick out the next moment of our lives,</br>
        we listen for the faintest tinkling of hope,</br>
        and bear witness of this time as sentinels ourselves.</p>
      </div>
