        <h2>Royal Typewriter</h2>
        <p>Taken on homemade view camera and processed with a mixture of clove tea developer solution on Ilford positive paper.</p>
      </div>
      <div id="description2">
        <h2>Jeweler's Clock</h2>
        <p>One of the first images taken on my homemade view camera on RC Negative Paper and scanned, processed with caffenol &emdash; a solution made from instant coffee, ascorbic acid (vitamin C), and laundry soda.</p>
      </div>
      <div id="description3">
        <h2>Loveland First Presbyterian</h2>
        <p>100+ year old church building in downtown Loveland, taken on 1910 Kodak Folding Autographic Brownie Camera on 116 roll film. Processed with caffenol and scanned from negative.</p>
      </div>
      <div id="description4">
        <h2>Loveland Feed & Grain</h2>
        <p>This image of the Loveland Feed & Grain building was taken on a pinhole camera on 4x5 format film and processed with caffenol.</p>
      </div>
      <div id="description5">
        <h2>Pulliam Building</h2>
        <p>The Loveland Pulliam building, a community center undergoing renovation. This image was taken on pinhole camera using 4x5 film and processed using caffenol.</p>
